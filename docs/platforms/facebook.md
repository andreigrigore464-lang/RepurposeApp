# Platform Constraints: Facebook

## 1. Platform Identity
- **Identifier:** `facebook`
- **Supported Formats:** `text-post`, `card`
- **Native Delivery Method:** Copy text to clipboard, download PNG for graphic card.

---

## 2. Technical Constraints

| Property | Limit / Rule | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Max Character Count** | Up to 63,206 characters | Practical editorial ceiling: 800–1,500 characters. |
| **Visible Truncation (The Fold)** | ~400 characters (approx. 5 lines) | Clear thesis statement required upfront. |
| **Hashtags** | 0 to 2 maximum | Hashtags are seldom used on Facebook; keep minimal or omit. |
| **Images** | Single image for `card` | 1200x630 (1.91:1) or 1080x1080 (1:1). |
| **Carousel Support** | Limited without ad manager | Carousels not supported for standard organic export; use single `card` or `text-post`. |
| **Links** | Clickable in post body | Post body can include full article URL. |

---

## 3. Formatting & Styling Rules
- Narrative flow: Works well with storytelling setups, conversational hooks, and problem-to-solution structures.
- Clear paragraph spacing with double returns.
